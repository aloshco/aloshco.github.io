Privacy Policy

Sahrora · last updated 29 August 2026

This policy describes what Sahrora collects, why, and what stays on your phone. It covers the Sahrora app on iOS and Android.

You do not need to give us anything to play

Sahrora creates an anonymous account for you automatically. It is a random identifier with no email address, phone number or real name attached. You can play the whole app without ever signing in.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple — so your progress survives a new phone — our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) then holds the name and email address that service gives us.

What we store about you

  • Your profile: the display name you type, the avatar you pick, a country code, and a six-character friend code.
  • Your results: Arcade runs played, points, accuracy, achievements, weekly scores and medals, so leaderboards, the friends league and your stats screen work.
  • Coins: a ledger of the coins you earn in the app, so a reward cannot be claimed twice.
  • Rooms: who is in a room you host or join, their scores, and any chat messages sent in it. Rooms are deleted after they expire.
  • Friends: the friend codes you add, and the friend requests you send and receive.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push token for your device. We store a hash of it as the record's name, not the token itself.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google. We receive a receipt confirming what you bought — never your card details.

Your country is a setting, not a location

Sahrora uses a two-letter country code to show a regional leaderboard. It starts from your device's language and region settings and you can change it in You → Account. We do not use GPS, and we do not look up your IP address.

What stays on your phone

Your progress in the Learn section — lesson stars, stickers, levels cleared, the daily sprint streak and your personal bests — is stored on your device only. So are your Arcade best scores, the games you picked for your runs, and your settings. The only thing Learn sends anywhere is the coins it awards, which go to the coin ledger above.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and Firebase Crashlytics to find crashes and performance data (app start-up and slow-frame timings). These record events such as "a run started" together with your app language, whether you subscribe, and roughly how many games you have played. They do not receive your name or email.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Play tab, a video you can choose to watch in exchange for something in the game, and one short interstitial ad before an Arcade run. There are no ads anywhere in the Learn section, and no ads during a game, a question or a results screen. Sahrora+ and the one-time Remove Ads purchase remove the banner and the interstitial.

Every ad request Sahrora makes is tagged for child-directed treatment and limited to general-audience (G) content. Because of that tag, Google does not use your advertising identifier to personalise the ads you see.

On iOS, Sahrora asks whether you will allow tracking. If you say no, or never answer, nothing in the app changes: ads are already non-personalised, and the identifier is not used for advertising. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's own privacy policy covers what it does with ad data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, your photo library or your microphone. Sahrora's Kids lessons speak to you using your device's own text-to-speech; nothing is recorded.
  • The camera is used for one thing — scanning a friend's room QR code — and that image is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Children

Sahrora is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. The Learn section includes lessons written for young children; everything it records stays on the device, it shows no ads, and it never asks a child for a name, an age or any other information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Every ad shown anywhere in the app is requested with child-directed treatment, as described above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: You → Account → Delete account. That removes your sign-in, your profile (name, avatar, country, friend code), your friend connections — including your entry in your friends' own lists — your notification token and your room history. A few records are kept without your name or any way to reach you: the coin ledger, medals, weekly scores and the daily-limit counter, because they protect leaderboard integrity and prevent rewards being claimed twice. You can also email us at the address below and we will help.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — accounts, database, analytics, crash reporting, notifications
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase receipts
  • Apple and Google — payment processing
