Privacy Policy

Sahrora · last updated 14 October 2026

This policy describes what Sahrora collects, why, and what stays on your phone. It covers the Sahrora app on iOS and Android.

You do not need to give us anything to play

Sahrora creates an anonymous account for you automatically. It is a random identifier with no email address, phone number or real name attached. You can play the whole app without ever signing in.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple — so your progress survives a new phone — our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) then holds the name and email address that service gives us.

What we store about you

  • Your profile: the display name you type and the avatar you pick.
  • Your results: Arcade runs and Quiz Night games played, points, accuracy, medals and achievements, so your stats screen works and your progress follows your account to a new phone.
  • Coins: a ledger of the coins you earn in the app, so a reward cannot be claimed twice.
  • Rooms: who is in a room you host or join, by display name, and their scores. Rooms are deleted after they expire. There is no free-text chat in rooms — players can only send a few fixed phrases (like "Get ready!") and sounds.
  • Question reports: if you tap Report this question, we store which question it was, the reason you chose and your account's random identifier, so we can fix the question. Reports are never shown to other players.
  • Player reports and blocks: if you report or block another player, we store the report (room code, the player's display name and the reason) so we can act on it. Blocks are kept with your account.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google. We receive a receipt confirming what you bought — never your card details.

Location

We do not use GPS. The app reads your device's region setting to choose the app language and to put the question categories for your country first; that region is saved with your profile and your stats. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may work out an approximate location (such as your country or city) from your internet (IP) address — see Google's policy in the Advertising section.

What stays on your phone

Your progress in the Learn section — lesson stars, stickers, levels cleared, the daily sprint streak and your personal bests — is stored on your device only. So are your Arcade best scores, the games you picked for your runs, and your settings. The only thing Learn sends anywhere is the coins it awards, which go to the coin ledger above.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and Firebase Crashlytics to find crashes and performance data (app start-up and slow-frame timings). These record events such as "a run started" together with your app language, whether you subscribe, and roughly how many games you have played. They do not receive your name or email.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a video you can choose to watch — to play beyond the free daily allowance, or to open some question categories for 24 hours — and short interstitial ads after a solo game, after a game or race in a room has finished, and after you join a room. There are no ads anywhere in the Learn section, no banners, and none during a game or a question. Sahrora+ and the one-time Remove Ads purchase remove the interstitial ads.

Every ad request Sahrora makes is tagged for child-directed treatment and limited to general-audience (G) content. Because of that tag, Google does not use your advertising identifier to personalise the ads you see.

On iOS, Sahrora asks whether you will allow tracking. If you allow it, Google may receive your device's advertising identifier with ad requests, to measure ads and to stop the same ad repeating; because of the child-directed tag it is not used to personalise ads. If you say no, or never answer, the identifier is not shared and nothing in the app changes. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's own privacy policy covers what it does with ad data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications

Sahrora sends notifications only if you allow them. They are calls from other players in a room you are in — for example "Call everyone" from the host, or "Get ready!" from a player — and an optional weekly reminder, which stays off unless you turn it on. To deliver them we store a notification token for each of your devices with your account; it is deleted when you sign out or delete your account. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, your photo library or your microphone. Sahrora's Kids lessons speak to you using your device's own text-to-speech; nothing is recorded.
  • The camera is used for one thing — scanning a room's QR code to join it — and that image is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Children

Sahrora is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. The Learn section includes lessons written for young children; everything it records stays on the device, it shows no ads, and it never asks a child for a name, an age or any other information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Every ad shown anywhere in the app is requested with child-directed treatment, as described above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: You → Account → Delete account. That removes your sign-in and your profile (name and avatar), and deletes your notification tokens. A few records are kept without your name or any way to reach you: the coin ledger and coin wallet, your medals, your scores and the daily-limit counter, because they prevent rewards and free plays being claimed twice. You can also email us at the address below and we will help.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — accounts, database, notifications (Cloud Messaging), analytics, crash reporting
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase receipts
  • Apple and Google — payment processing
