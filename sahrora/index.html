Sahrora

Party trivia, twenty-odd mini-games, and a mental-maths and colour-mixing course — in English, Arabic, French and Spanish.

What is in it

  • Arcade — a run of ten short mini-games, solo or raced against friends.
  • Quiz Master — one phone, the whole room, two to eight players.
  • Learn — mental-maths tricks and colour mixing, as lessons with practice and a daily challenge. Free, and with no ads on any of its screens.

Languages

Every screen is translated into English, Arabic (fully right-to-left), French and Spanish.

Price

Free to play, with ads. An optional Sahrora+ subscription removes the ads and lifts the daily limits, and a one-off purchase removes the ads on their own. Nothing in the game is paywalled mid-play.
