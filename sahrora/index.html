Sahrora

Nineteen arcade games to race your friends through, and a Learn world for kids and grown-ups — in English, Arabic, French and Spanish.

What is in it

  • Arcade — nineteen short games, dealt ten at a time; play a run on your own or race friends through the same ten.
  • Rooms — start a room with one tap, share a code or a QR, and race everyone on their own phone.
  • Learn — Kids — six drawn worlds (numbers, colours, shapes, the clock, everyday life, puzzles), each lesson spoken aloud.
  • Learn — Grown-ups — fast maths, memory games and real-life drills. The first spots of every world are free, the rest is Sahrora+, and no Learn screen ever shows an ad.

Languages

Every screen is translated into English, Arabic (fully right-to-left), French and Spanish.

Price

Free to play, with ads around the Arcade and none in Learn. An optional Sahrora+ subscription removes the ads, lifts the daily limits, lets you pick your own arcade mix and opens every lesson; a one-off purchase removes the ads on their own. Nothing in the game is paywalled mid-play.
