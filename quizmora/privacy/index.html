Privacy Policy

Quizmora · last updated 22 August 2026

This policy describes what Quizmora collects, why, and what stays on your phone. It covers the Quizmora app on iOS and Android.

You do not need to give us anything to play

Quizmora creates an anonymous account for you automatically. It is a random identifier with no email address, phone number or real name attached. You can play the whole game without ever signing in.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple — so your progress survives a new phone — our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) then holds the name and email address that service gives us.

What we store about you

  • Your profile: the display name you type, the avatar you pick, a country code, and a six-character friend code.
  • Your results: games played, points, accuracy, achievements and weekly scores, so leaderboards and your stats screen work.
  • Live Quiz rooms: who is in a room, their scores, and any chat messages sent in it. Rooms are deleted after they expire.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push token for your device. We store a hash of it as the record's name, not the token itself.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple or Google. We receive a receipt confirming what you bought — never your card details.

Your country comes from your device's language settings

Quizmora uses a two-letter country code to filter public rooms and to show a regional leaderboard. It is read from your device's language and region settings. We do not use GPS, and we do not look up your IP address. You can change the country yourself in Settings.

What never leaves your phone

The whole Learn section is stored on your device only. The names and age bands of the learners you add, their lesson progress, their streaks and their results are never uploaded to us and never leave the phone. So are your Arcade best scores, your settings and your game preferences.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and Firebase Crashlytics to find crashes. These record events such as "a game started" together with your app language, whether you subscribe, and roughly how many games you have played. They do not receive your name or email.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on two screens, and videos you can choose to watch in exchange for something in the game. There are no ads anywhere in the Learn section, and no ads during a game, a question or a results screen.

On iOS, Quizmora asks whether you will allow tracking. If you say no, or never answer, we do not use your device's advertising identifier and you see non-personalised ads instead. Nothing else in the app changes. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's own privacy policy covers what it does with ad data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, your photo library or your microphone.
  • The camera is used for one thing — scanning a friend's room QR code — and that image is processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Children

Quizmora is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. The Learn section includes material written for younger learners; everything it records stays on the device, as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: You → Account → Delete account. That removes your profile, your stats and your friend connections. You can also email us at the address below and we will do it for you.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — accounts, database, analytics, crash reporting, notifications
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase receipts
  • Apple and Google — payment processing
