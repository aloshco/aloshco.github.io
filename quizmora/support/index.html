Quizmora Support

Something not working, or a question? Email mohammad.sami.apps@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Before you write

If something went wrong in a game, the app keeps a log that makes it much easier to find: You → Settings → Export logs. Attaching it to your email saves a lot of back and forth.

Common questions

  • I paid and nothing unlocked. Try You → Settings → Restore purchases. If that does not do it, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • I want to cancel Quizmora+. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us: You → Settings → Manage subscription opens the right page.
  • I changed phone and lost my progress. Progress follows the account you signed in with. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and it comes back. Learn progress is stored on the device itself and does not transfer.
  • How do I delete my account? You → Account → Delete account.
